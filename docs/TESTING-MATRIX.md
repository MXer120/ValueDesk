# Testing matrix: algo orders and copy trading

Where each edge case is tested, so a tester knows what is covered and what to try by hand. Written
2026-10-29. Every **unit** and **audit** test below runs offline in CI (`node deploy/ci/run-tests.mjs`, which
finds every `*.test.ts` by itself). The **live suite** (`apps/executor/tools/live-test`, real money on SoDEX
mainnet) is never run by CI; its scenarios are named by id, from `scenarios/algos.ts`, `copy.ts` or
`setup.ts`.

Paths: `ce/` = `packages/copy-engine/test/`, `ex/` = `apps/executor/test/`, `web/` = `apps/web/src/`.
Tests marked **new** were added with this matrix (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, `ce/edge-copy.test.ts`,
`ex/edge-cases.test.ts`, `web/algo/edge-tabLife.test.ts`, `web/edge-lazyChunk.test.ts`). A name starting
"BUG" is a test that failed until its fix. A "FINDING" is a `test.todo` that holds the failing case: it
runs, it fails, and CI reports it as a todo, not a failure.

## Bugs the new tests found

**Fixed** (small and contained):

- **The brake held back the exits of what filled while it was on.** `brake.ts` promises "an algo's exits are
  still placed", but `AlgoRuntime.step` (`packages/copy-engine/src/algo/runtime.ts`) returned straight after
  the brake's hold, before `syncExits`, and a fill of a resting child only places exits by itself once the
  algo has ended. A clip or ladder level that filled during a brake stood without its stop-loss until the
  brake was lifted. Now the braked step still keeps the exits in line with what filled (it sends nothing
  else). Test: `ce/edge-algo.test.ts` "BUG brake: a resting child that fills while the brake is on gets its
  exits (brake.ts: "an algo's exits are still placed")".
- **A copied position SoDEX made smaller kept its loss out of the copy's books.** `refreshFollower`
  (`packages/copy-engine/src/runtime.ts`) took SoDEX's smaller size but booked nothing for the part that went (a
  whole close outside is booked at the mark): the copy's equity, its drawdown stop and a proportional copy's sizing
  read a loss SoDEX had taken as never taken. Now the part that went is booked at the mark. Test:
  `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts` "liquidated in part ..." ("BUG: the part SoDEX took is booked").
- **After a position closed outside it, the copier opened at whatever leverage SoDEX had.** The copier remembers
  the leverage it set per wallet and market and sets it again only when its own rule changes, so a leverage raised
  by hand on sodex.com before a liquidation stayed for the copy's next open - past the copy's cap, the EEA's 2x
  included. Now a position closed outside the copier forgets it: the next open sets the copy's leverage first. Test:
  `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts` "liquidated in full (isolated) ..." ("BUG: the copy's leverage is set again").
- **An algo's exits outlived a liquidated position, and were placed again.** An ended algo never looked at its
  position: exits left on SoDEX after a takeover (or a close by hand) would close a later position on that market,
  and one SoDEX expired with nothing to reduce counted as a refusal, so a new pair went out. Now an ended live
  algo holds its resting exits against the position (`algo/runtime.ts` watchExits: every minute, a read that left at
  least 3 s after they were placed; once before a refused pair goes out again; once after SoDEX cancelled them).
  Flat or on the other side, with no exit filled (SoDEX's order history asked first), what rests is cancelled,
  nothing is placed again, the log says why and the trader gets an error notice (`closed`, "position closed
  outside it"). Unreadable, nothing changes. Tests: `ce/edge-algo.test.ts` "BUG liquidated, algo: ..." and the
  cases after it.
- **A copied position SoDEX made smaller was adopted without a word.** Now the part that went outside the
  copier is told: an activity line (`reduced-outside`, its size) and a notice (`reduced`, ref
  `<copy>:<market>`; an error for a partial liquidation, a warning otherwise, info for the copy's own stop). The
  copier remembers its reduce-only orders for 10 min (`recentShrink`, as `recentGrow` for opens) and what of
  each the stream showed filled, so a reduction of its own whose fill was missed is not told; what it does not
  explain is. What the copy does next is unchanged (owner's call below). Tests: `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts`
  "BUG partial liquidation: ..." and the cases after it.
- **A liquidation past the copy's stop-loss level was told as the stop.** `closedWhy` named the reason from the
  mark alone. Now `outsideWhy` reads SoDEX's fills of the market since the last account read (GET
  `.../trades`, weight 25, only when a position changed outside the copier): a `liquidation:` fill is a
  liquidation (an error, "liquidated in the copy of ..."), the copy's stop's client id filled is its stop (info),
  and anything else - unreadable fills included - is closed outside (an error). The stop is never named without
  its fill. Tests: `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts` "BUG stop-loss vs liquidation: ..." and the cases after it.
- **The live suite could not start a live run.** Its own writes went through the engine's SoDEX client, which since
  go-live round 3 sends only orders, cancels and leverage (`ALLOWED_WRITES`): the key registration and the builder
  approval of the start were refused before they left (and the liquidation scenarios' margin change would be too).
  `exchange.ts` now sends those straight to SoDEX's gateway. Test: `ex/live-test-runner.test.ts` "the harness's own
  writes: ...".

**Findings** (left open, each a `test.todo` holding the failing case):

1. **Close-only region holds closes too** (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, "FINDING region: ..."). A live algo of a
   wallet whose region is close-only (US, UK, Canada) is held whatever it does, a reduce-only time-sliced
   close or a trailing stop included, because `step` holds on `policy.allowed === false` before it looks
   at what the algo sends. The copy planner lets reductions and closes through for a blocked copy. Which
   one is right is the owner's call: a legal rule, not a code fix.
2. **Delisted market, algo** (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, "FINDING delisted: ..."). When a market leaves SoDEX's
   list mid-run, the algo ends `failed` with the reason, but its resting children are not cancelled:
   `end()` and `pullResting()` need the market's record to cancel and return without it. Unconfirmed: whether
   SoDEX cancels them itself on a delisting.
3. **Delisted market, copy** - closed 2026-10-29 (owner: a delisting is the follower's to act on). A copy
   that still holds a position on a market SoDEX no longer lists is still not closed or traded by the copier,
   and now the follower is told once: a warn notice ("delisted") and a line in the copy's activity
   (`runtime.ts` noteDelisted). Now a test: `ce/edge-copy.test.ts` "delisted: ...", `ex/not-on-sodex.test.ts`.
   Still open: what SoDEX itself does with such a position.
4. **Liquidated, algo** - closed 2026-10-29 (fixed above, "An algo's exits outlived a liquidated position").
   Now a test: `ce/edge-algo.test.ts` "BUG liquidated, algo: ...". Still open: an algo still running when its
   position goes is not checked (its own fills move the position while it runs).
5. **Partial liquidation, copy** - closed 2026-10-29 (fixed above, "A copied position SoDEX made smaller was
   adopted without a word"). Now a test: `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts` "BUG partial liquidation: ...".
6. **Stop-loss vs liquidation, copy** - closed 2026-10-29 (fixed above, "A liquidation past the copy's
   stop-loss level was told as the stop"). Now a test: `ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts` "BUG stop-loss vs
   liquidation: ...". Still open: a whole close of the copier's own whose fill the stream missed is still told
   as closed outside (the reduction memory is used for a position made smaller only).

**Owner's call** (pinned, not judged): after a partial liquidation the copy keeps its size per leader unit, so on
the leader's next add it buys back to its target - the leader's add and the part SoDEX took - within its leverage
room on its booked equity (`ce/edge-liquidation.test.ts`, "liquidated in part"). Proportional copies size from the
equity, which now books the part that went; fixed-amount and fixed-ratio ones do not shrink their target.
