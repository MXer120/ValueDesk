# Testing matrix: algo orders and copy trading

Where each edge case is tested, so a tester knows what is covered and what to try by hand. Written
2026-10-29. Every **unit** and **audit** test below runs offline in CI (`node deploy/ci/run-tests.mjs`, which
finds every `*.test.ts` by itself). The **live suite** (`apps/executor/tools/live-test`, real money on SoDEX
mainnet) is never run by CI; its scenarios are named by id, from `scenarios/algos.ts`, `copy.ts` or
`setup.ts`.

Paths: `ce/` = `packages/copy-engine/test/`, `ex/` = `apps/executor/test/`, `web/` = `apps/web/src/`.
Tests marked **new** were added with this matrix (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, `ce/edge-copy.test.ts`,
`ex/edge-cases.test.ts`, `web/algo/edge-tabLife.test.ts`, `web/edge-lazyChunk.test.ts`). A name starting
"BUG" is a test that failed until its fix. A "FINDING" is a `test.todo` that holds the failing case: it
runs, it fails, and CI reports it as a todo, not a failure.

## Bugs the new tests found

**Fixed** (small and contained):

- **The brake held back the exits of what filled while it was on.** `brake.ts` promises "an algo's exits are
  still placed", but `AlgoRuntime.step` (`packages/copy-engine/src/algo/runtime.ts`) returned straight after
  the brake's hold, before `syncExits`, and a fill of a resting child only places exits by itself once the
  algo has ended. A clip or ladder level that filled during a brake stood without its stop-loss until the
  brake was lifted. Now the braked step still keeps the exits in line with what filled (it sends nothing
  else). Test: `ce/edge-algo.test.ts` "BUG brake: a resting child that fills while the brake is on gets its
  exits (brake.ts: "an algo's exits are still placed")".

**Findings** (left open, each a `test.todo` holding the failing case):

1. **Close-only region holds closes too** (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, "FINDING region: ..."). A live algo of a
   wallet whose region is close-only (US, UK, Canada) is held whatever it does, a reduce-only time-sliced
   close or a trailing stop included, because `step` holds on `policy.allowed === false` before it looks
   at what the algo sends. The copy planner lets reductions and closes through for a blocked copy. Which
   one is right is the owner's call: a legal rule, not a code fix.
2. **Delisted market, algo** (`ce/edge-algo.test.ts`, "FINDING delisted: ..."). When a market leaves SoDEX's
   list mid-run, the algo ends `failed` with the reason, but its resting children are not cancelled:
   `end()` and `pullResting()` need the market's record to cancel and return without it. Unconfirmed: whether
   SoDEX cancels them itself on a delisting.
3. **Delisted market, copy** (`ce/edge-copy.test.ts`, "FINDING delisted: ..."). A copy that still holds a
   position on a market SoDEX no longer lists is neither closed nor told; `closeStrays` skips a market it
   cannot find. Same open question about what SoDEX does on a delisting.
