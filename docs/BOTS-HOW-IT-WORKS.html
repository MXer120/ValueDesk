1. The whole picture: who does what

  • A bot is an algo record with bot: true, kind mm (market maker) or grid (packages/copy-engine/src/algo). It lives in the executor, never in the browser.
  • The trading key: a SoDEX agent key you approved once. It can place and cancel orders and set leverage; it cannot withdraw. The executor never has your wallet's own key.
  • Every change (start, stop, pause, save, restart, VIP move) is a request your wallet signs. Reads use a 30-day read-only session token, so you sign in once a month per executor, not per action.
  • The step: the executor runs every bot every 500 ms (ALGO_TICK_MS) and also on each book or trade update of its market.
  • Restart safe: bots, their orders and their books are stored in the executor's database and resume after a restart. While the executor is down, resting quotes stay on SoDEX (SoDEX has no cancel-on-disconnect).

The trading key

  • Approved once on SoDEX; it has an expiry. Wallet notices 24 h and 1 h before expiry, and a Renew banner on the Bots pages.
  • From 10 minutes before expiry: every bot pulls its quotes and opens nothing; reduce-only closes still go out until the key expires. Not verified live
  • After expiry (or revocation) the bots are held with "key" until a new key is approved.

Every 500 ms and on every market update, each market maker runs this. (runtime.ts botGuard, stepMm; bots.ts mmQuotes)

Step 1 runs before every hold: a held or paused bot still stops at its loss limit, end time or volume target.

6. Where a quote goes (price rules)

adding bid is not quoted. C. Long and Binance 99.99: the unwinding ask is still quoted, never below fair. D. No fresh Binance price: held (section 5, step 8). Book maker: no oracle, cases B-D do not apply. Skew (S1, skewTicks 1): adding side steps back ticks back = round(|holding ÷ cap| × skewTicks), adding side only. Example: cap $150, long $90, skew 1 → round(0.6 × 1) = 1 → the bid sits 1 tick below the best bid. The ask stays at the touch.
  • A quote is never at or through the other side of the book (post-only would be refused).
  • Size = order size ÷ price, rounded to the market's lot, raised to SoDEX's minimum if that is larger.
  • Edge guard (edgeBps, optional) measures from the safer of mid and mark.
  • Queue guard (optional): the adding side is skipped while our share of the queue at our price is below the setting.
  • Move guard (optional): after the mid moved this many ticks in 2 s, new adding quotes wait.

7. Holding cap and unwinding (reduce-only)

"Holding" is what the bot holds from its own fills (buys − sells), not the account's position. (bots.ts mmQuotes, bookInventory)

not quoted; only the ask (unwinding) is. Unwinding ask: reduce-only, size ≤ what it holds ($95 → at most $95, so it ends flat, never short). A holding below SoDEX's minimum order cannot be quoted; it stays until the stop closes it.
One real bot per market (your decision 2026-11-09, reverting the evening before): a bot starts only where you hold no position and run no other bot, copy or algo order. A bot inherits a position only from the bot it replaces (Instant restart). Its holding is checked against your account every 30 s: closed or reduced outside it (by hand, a stop, a liquidation) is booked closed at the mark with a notice, and it quotes on from what is left; a position you add by hand is left alone and said once.

Trading hours in detail

  • Windows are set in any time zone: your own (chosen hours stay those hours of your clock across daylight saving), New York, London, UTC, or any other IANA zone. NYSE and LSE holidays are computed; other zones count Monday to Friday as trading days.
  • Two modes: skip these hours (default) or trade only in these hours (quietOnly).
  • The page shows each window also in your own clock. Half-hour time zones (e.g. UTC+5:30) are shown exactly.
  • Explore's hour profile, its read time, Started on Overview and the latency chart show your own clock (built 2026-11-08, 2c50c9e9).

Who are 1002 and 23342?

SoDEX account 1002 trades in large, slow, price-insensitive flow: being filled by it is cheap for a maker ("uninformed flow"). Account 23342 takes quotes just before the price moves: being filled by it costs about 0.86 bp ("informed flow"). Explore shows each market's split. Figures from our maker runs 2026-11-04.

9. The grid

  • Levels: linear (equal price steps) or geometric (equal % steps), rounded to the tick. At start, buys below the price, sells above, the level nearest the price left empty (the gap).
  • Size per level: order size ÷ level price, at least SoDEX's minimum.
  • All levels are post-only. A level whose price the market is at or through waits. A refused level waits 3 s, doubling to 60 s. At most 50 orders per batch.
  • Applies: spread limit, loss limit, end time, volume target, leverage, margin. Not for grids: trading hours, news gate, flow gate, oracle, holding cap.
  • Margin set aside: levels × order ÷ leverage.

All three end through the bot's On stop setting (section 11). The bot page's Limits card shows how close it is to each (built 2026-11-08): loss used of the limit (amber past 50%, red past 80%), time left, volume of the target.

Leverage

  • Set on the market before the bot's first opening order. Only SoDEX's market maximum caps it (no region cap, no 3x default). Auto (default): the lowest whole leverage at which the bot's margin fits your free margin with a tenth to spare; 1x for practice. Set by hand, Auto turns off; Auto puts it back.
  • If SoDEX refuses the leverage change, the bot opens nothing and asks again after 1 min, doubling to 15 min (no more every 500 ms); reduce-only orders still go out. Leverage saves in place on a running bot.
  • SoDEX leverage is per market per account, so it also applies to your own position there and to every bot on that market (they share it). SoDEX accepted a higher leverage on an open position in our runs; a lower one not verified live.

Margin a bot sets aside

market maker: margin = (max holding + 2 × real quote size × levels) ÷ leverage
grid: margin = levels × real order size ÷ leverage
Worked example. Balanced market maker, order $50, cap $150, 1 level, 3×: (150 + 2 × 50 × 1) ÷ 3 = $83.33.
Same on a market with a $100 minimum order: (150 + 2 × 100) ÷ 3 = $116.67, but a $150 cap is under two minimum quotes, so the page warns; under one minimum quote it is refused.
Grid Balanced, 16 levels × $25 at 3×: 400 ÷ 3 = $133.33.

14. The numbers on the pages

volume = buy value + sell value
holding = bought qty − sold qty (+ taken-over qty)
gross = sell value − buy value − taken-over value + holding × mark
net PnL = gross − SoDEX fees − ValueDesk fees
cost = −net ÷ volume × 10,000 bp ( = −net ÷ volume × $1,000,000 per $1M )
Worked example. Buy 1 ETH at 3,000.00, sell 1 at 3,001.00. Volume $6,001. Gross +$1.00. SoDEX 1.2 bp × 6,001 = $0.72; bot fee 0.5 bp = $0.30. Net = 1.00 − 0.72 − 0.30 = −$0.02. Cost = 0.02 ÷ 6,001 × 10,000 = 0.03 bp ($3 per $1M).

15. Practice bots

  • Same engine, same rules, live SoDEX book and trades; nothing is sent. Fees are booked as if real.
  • A practice order fills only when a real trade prints through its price (not at it). Each print's size is shared first-come across practice orders.
  • No queue position is modelled, so practice is still somewhat better than live.
  • Practice skips beta, capacity, market and margin checks; loss limit optional.
  • "Run with real orders" opens New bot with the same settings.

16. VIP servers and the move

VIP executor: AWS Tokyo, closer to SoDEX (orders take effect faster). Eligible: $1M own 14-day volume, or a VIP program (beta 30 days).

Both ways (built 2026-11-08, 46da38f0):
  • The VIP executor asks the API server about each wallet whose bots run there every 10 minutes, twice a moment apart; a failed read moves nobody.
  • A wallet no longer VIP (under $1M in 14 days and no VIP program) moves back: same steps, key sealed to the pinned EXECUTOR_STD_PUBKEY, sent to EXECUTOR_STD_URL.
  • At most one move back per wallet per UTC day. Moving up is never limited (it still needs VIP).
  • Round trips work: a bot that comes home replaces its own ended record. The wallet gets a notice on the standard side; the page follows by itself, using the read token the move brought.
  • Production needs on the VIP box: EXECUTOR_STD_URL, EXECUTOR_STD_PUBKEY (else nobody is moved back). On the standard box: EXECUTOR_VIP_PUBKEY.
